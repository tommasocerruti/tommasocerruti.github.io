I’m an MSc student in Computer Science at ETH Zurich, majoring in AI/ML. My work focuses primarily on the safety, alignment, and evaluation of AI agents. I love building systems, measuring how they behave, and improving their capabilities and efficiency.

Research interests

  • Generalization to unfamiliar tasks, inference budgets, agent scaffolds, and deployment environments.
  • Harness and memory failures, identity and provenance, delegation, multi-agent interactions, and endogenous failures.
  • Learned computation, programmatic mechanisms, efficient sequence models, and interfaces for intelligent systems.

Background

I interned as a SWE on Amazon’s Alexa AI team, where I built a service to improve NER. I earned a BEng in Computer Engineering from Politecnico di Torino and was selected for its Talents Program. I also received the Agon Scholarship for national and international rowing achievements, including an indoor rowing world record.
