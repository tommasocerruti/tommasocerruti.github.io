I’m an MSc student in Computer Science at ETH Zurich, specializing in Machine Intelligence. My work focuses primarily on the safety, alignment, and evaluation of AI agents. My approach is empirical and engineering-driven: I enjoy building systems, measuring how they behave, and improving their capabilities and efficiency.

Research interests

  • Robust evaluation: eval-aware behavior, reward hacking, and broader alignment failures.
  • Agent security: memory poisoning/laundering and risks from long-running state.
  • Multi-agent safety: manipulation, adversarial attacks, and endogenous failures.

Background

Previously, I interned as a software engineer on Amazon’s Alexa AI team, where I built a service to improve named entity recognition. I earned a BEng in Computer Engineering from Politecnico di Torino and was selected for its Young Talents Program. I also received the Agon Scholarship for national and international rowing achievements, including an indoor rowing world record.
